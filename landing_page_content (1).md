## How It Works

Complaints Don't Wait. Neither Do We.

1. A citizen spots a problem and files a report
2. The system reads it, categorises it, and flags the priority
3. The closest available worker gets assigned automatically
4. The worker is notified and the citizen is updated
5. Once resolved, the record is closed and saved

From report to resolution — without anyone having to chase it.
